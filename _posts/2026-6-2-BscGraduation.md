On June 1, 2026, Boyi Fu graduated from Macau University of Science and Technology with a Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science. He was deeply honored to receive the University's Honor Cord, a distinction awarded to only four graduates across the Faculty of Innovation Engineering (FIE). This recognition marked a meaningful conclusion to his undergraduate journey and reflected the generous support he received from his supervisor, friends, classmates, and family.<br/>
<br/>

Boyi Fu sincerely thanked his supervisor, Prof. Junxiang Yang, for his patient guidance and encouragement in scientific research. He was also grateful to all the teachers and friends who accompanied him throughout his undergraduate studies and made these four years both productive and memorable.<br/>

As graduation brought this chapter to a close, Boyi Fu said farewell to his friends and teachers at M.U.S.T. and prepared to move forward to the next stage of his life. He will carry with him the knowledge, friendships, and research experiences gained during his undergraduate years.<br/>
